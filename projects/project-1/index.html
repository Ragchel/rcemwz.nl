Project 01 · The collection

My Pokémon
TCG binder

A page-by-page home for my collection. Open the binder and have a look around, just as you would in real life.

Open the binder ↓

I wanted my cards to be as enjoyable to browse online as they are laid out on the table.

This digital version follows my 16-pocket Vault X binder: four cards across, four down, with every numbered pocket kept in its real position. Empty pockets stay visible, because a collection in progress is part of the fun.

One pocket at a time

I can add to this binder without rebuilding the page. Each image is matched to its pocket number, so the digital collection keeps the same order as the physical one.
